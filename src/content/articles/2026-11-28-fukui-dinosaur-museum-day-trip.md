**Short answer:** Yes, it works as a day trip, but **book a timed ticket first**. The Fukui Prefectural Dinosaur Museum sells entry by date and time slot, and the museum says it sells walk-up tickets on the day only if any are left. From Kanazawa, take the Hokuriku Shinkansen to **Fukui**. From there it's either the **Echizen Railway** to Katsuyama plus a short bus ride, or a **direct bus** from Fukui Station that takes about an hour.

The museum is in **Katsuyama**, a town in the mountains east of Fukui City. It is one of the biggest dinosaur museums in Asia, and it's the reason many travellers with kids make the trip from Kanazawa. Fukui is a different prefecture, but on the Shinkansen it is closer than it sounds.

*Information checked on the museum's official website, the Echizen Railway site, the Keifuku Bus booking site (happybus.jp) and the JR West timetable on November 28, 2026. Most of these pages are only in Japanese. Prices, timetables and closing days change, so check them again before you go.*

## Do I need to book a ticket?

Yes. The museum's Japanese site says that, in principle, tickets are **bought in advance for a set date and time** (日時指定, *nichiji shitei*, "date and time designated"). This is the rule that catches people out, because many older English guides were written before the booking system started.

What the official pages say:

- Tickets go on sale **60 days** before the visit date.
- The English page links to **asoview**, a Japanese ticket site, for online tickets. You can pay by credit card there.
- **Same-day tickets** are sold at the ticket machine and the main reception desk, but only while they last. The museum warns that they can sell out on busy days such as the summer Obon holiday and three-day weekends.
- Phone bookings are not accepted.
- You can **leave and come back in** as many times as you like on the day of your ticket.

## How much does it cost?

Permanent exhibition prices from the official fee page:

| Visitor | Price |
|---|---|
| Adults | ¥1,000 |
| High school and university students | ¥800 |
| Elementary and junior high school students | ¥500 |
| Age 70 and over | ¥500 |
| Children under school age | Free |

Special exhibitions have a separate price. The museum also says that, because of the booking system, there are **no free-admission days for now**.

## When is it open?

- **Hours:** 9:00 to 17:00. **Last entry is 16:30**, including re-entry.
- **Closed:** the **2nd and 4th Wednesday** of each month (if that is a public holiday, it closes the next day instead), plus **December 31 and January 1**. It stays open every day during the summer holidays.
- The museum can also close for exhibit changes, so look at the calendar on the official site.

**Winter note:** the museum also has an outdoor site, the **Field Station** (野外恐竜博物館), where visitors look for fossils. The museum's top page says it has **closed for this season** and plans to reopen in mid to late April 2027. The main museum is open in winter.

## How do I get from Kanazawa to the museum?

### Step 1: Shinkansen to Fukui

Take the Hokuriku Shinkansen from Kanazawa toward Tsuruga and get off at **Fukui** (福井). On the JR West timetable, one Kagayaki train in the opposite direction took **33 minutes** from Fukui to Kanazawa, so plan on roughly half an hour each way. Not every train stops at Fukui, so check in a route app. My [guide to getting to Kanazawa](/articles/getting-to-kanazawa/) explains how the Shinkansen and reserved seats work.

### Step 2, option A: Echizen Railway + bus

The **Echizen Railway** (えちぜん鉄道, *Echizen tetsudō*) is a small local railway, not JR. Its **Katsuyama Eiheiji Line** runs from Fukui to **Katsuyama**, the last stop.

- **Changing at Fukui:** Echizen Railway has its own station next to JR Fukui. Its official page says you have to go down and out through the JR ticket gate, then walk to the Echizen Railway platform. It suggests allowing **about 10 minutes**.
- **Time:** the railway lists **54 minutes** from Fukui to Katsuyama during the daytime.
- **Fare:** ¥820 for an adult, one way.
- **IC cards:** Echizen Railway now accepts **ICOCA**. Its special day tickets cannot be paid for with an IC card, though. (More on cards in my [guide to paying in Kanazawa](/articles/paying-in-kanazawa/).)
- **From Katsuyama Station:** take the museum bus (恐竜博物館直通便) or a taxi. The museum's access page points you to the bus timetable on the Echizen Railway site. I could only find the timetable as an image, so I am not giving times here. Check it before you go.

The railway also sells a **museum transport set ticket** (恐竜博物館用交通セット券): a one-day Echizen Railway pass plus Katsuyama city buses, **¥1,700** for adults and ¥900 for children. It is sold at staffed station windows and by the train attendants. It **does not include museum admission**, so you still need your timed ticket.

### Step 2, option B: a direct bus from Fukui Station

Two bus services run straight from **JR Fukui Station's east exit** to the museum. Both are booked on Keifuku Bus's site, happybus.jp (Japanese).

- **Dinosaur Bus** (恐竜バス): about 60 minutes. In winter it runs on **weekends and holidays**, plus every day from December 28 to 30. The booking page says you need to **book each direction separately**, and seats on the day are sold only if any are left.
- **WOW RIDE Ikossa! Fukui** (WOW RIDE いこっさ！福井号): a newer bus with on-board VR entertainment, about 55 minutes, running most days with listed days off. It costs more than the Dinosaur Bus.

These buses don't need a transfer, but they cost more than the train and bus. Prices vary by ticket type, so check them on the booking page.

## What should I know before I go?

- **English:** the museum offers a **free exhibition guide app** with audio in English, Chinese (traditional and simplified) and Korean. Download it to your own phone. The museum also lends out 20 smartphones at reception. Bring earphones, or buy some at the museum shop.
- **Entrance:** if you have a web ticket, scan the QR code at the entry gate on the **3rd floor**.
- **Lunch:** there's a restaurant on the 3rd floor, but the museum warns that it gets **very crowded** on weekends and holidays.
- **Winter weather:** Katsuyama is in the mountains and usually gets more snow than Kanazawa. My [winter guide](/articles/kanazawa-winter-guide/) covers shoes and layers. The museum also says there is no covered walkway from the car park, so bring an umbrella.
- **Weekdays are calmer.** The museum says weekends and holidays are busy and there can be a short line before the 9:00 opening.

## Say it in Japanese

> **Kyōryū hakubutsukan made onegai shimasu.** (kyoh-ryoo hah-koo-boo-tsoo-kahn mah-deh oh-neh-gai shee-mass) — "To the Dinosaur Museum, please." Use this in a taxi from Katsuyama Station.

> **Kono basu wa kyōryū hakubutsukan ni ikimasu ka?** (koh-noh bah-soo wah kyoh-ryoo hah-koo-boo-tsoo-kahn nee ee-kee-mass kah) — "Does this bus go to the Dinosaur Museum?"

> **Yoyaku shite imasu.** (yoh-yah-koo shtay ee-mass) — "I have a booking." Show your QR code at the same time.

> **Katsuyama-eki yuki no basu wa nanji desu ka?** (kah-tsoo-yah-mah eh-kee yoo-kee noh bah-soo wah nahn-jee dess kah) — "What time is the bus to Katsuyama Station?"

## A simple day plan

1. **Book your timed ticket** online. Pick a morning slot so you are not rushing for the last entry at 16:30.
2. Leave big bags in Kanazawa. The [Kanazawa Station guide](/articles/kanazawa-station-guide/) explains where the lockers are.
3. Take a morning Shinkansen to Fukui (about half an hour).
4. Either change to the Echizen Railway for Katsuyama and take the bus, or board a booked direct bus at Fukui Station's east exit.
5. Allow at least a few hours in the museum. Your ticket lets you go out and come back in on the same day.
6. Return to Fukui and take the Shinkansen back to Kanazawa for dinner.

If you're choosing between day trips, the [Kaga Onsen day trip](/articles/kaga-onsen-day-trip/) is on the same Shinkansen line and pairs well with a cold day. The [Shirakawa-go day trip](/articles/shirakawa-go-day-trip/) goes the other way, by bus into the mountains.
