**Short answer:** for sightseeing, take the **Kanazawa Loop Bus** from **platform 7** at the East Exit of Kanazawa Station. It comes in two directions, the *right loop* and the *left loop*, and between them they stop at almost every famous place in the city. One ride is ¥220. If you will ride four times or more, the **1-day pass (¥800)** is worth it. The Loop Bus stops running around 6 p.m., so plan your evening return separately.

Kanazawa has no subway, and the main sights are spread out just far enough that buses make a big difference. This guide shows you which bus to take, where to get on and off, and what to say if you get confused.

*Information checked on Hokutetsu's (Hokuriku Railroad's) official website on November 16, 2026. Fares and timetables change, so check the [official Loop Bus page](https://www.hokutetsu.co.jp/en/round/) before you go.*

## Where to catch the bus at Kanazawa Station

Go out of the **East Exit**, the side with the big wooden gate. In front of you is the East Exit bus terminal. The Loop Bus leaves from **platform 7** (*nana-ban noriba*), for both directions.

There is a Hokutetsu bus information counter near platform 7. You can buy the 1-day pass there (more on that below).

## The Loop Bus: right loop and left loop

The **Kanazawa Loop Bus** (城下まち金沢周遊バス) is the bus most visitors use. It is a circular route from Kanazawa Station and back, in two directions:

| | Right loop (右回り) | Left loop (左回り) |
|---|---|---|
| How often | About every 15 minutes | About every 20 minutes |
| First bus from the station | 8:30 | 8:38 |
| Last bus from the station | 18:00 | 17:58 |
| Fare | ¥220 per ride (children ¥110) | ¥220 per ride (children ¥110) |

Both directions go to Omicho Market, Kenrokuen, the 21st Century Museum, Higashi Chaya and Korinbo. Only the **left loop** stops at Katamachi and at Hirokoji (for Nishi Chaya). So if you're unsure which one to take, look at where you want to go in the table below.

**Tip:** the right loop goes to Higashi Chaya first and Kenrokuen after it. The left loop goes the other way, through Korinbo and Katamachi first. Pick the direction that reaches your first stop sooner.

## How to pay

- **Cash:** ¥220 per ride on the Loop Bus. You pay when you get off, at the front by the driver. The fare box gives change for ¥1,000 notes and coins, but not for larger notes.
- **IC cards:** the Loop Bus accepts nationwide IC cards such as **Suica, PASMO and ICOCA**, as well as Hokutetsu's local card, ICa. Tap the reader when you get on and again when you get off.
- **Credit card touch payment:** Visa, Mastercard, JCB, AMEX, Diners, Discover and UnionPay contactless cards work on the Loop Bus and on Hokutetsu's local buses in the Kanazawa area. Tap when you get on and when you get off, just like an IC card.

One important difference: **regular Hokutetsu city buses (not the Loop Bus) do not accept Suica-type cards.** On those, use cash, a contactless credit card, ICa or the 1-day pass. For cash, cards and IC cards around the rest of the city, see my [guide to paying in Kanazawa](/articles/paying-in-kanazawa/).

## The 1-day pass

The **Kanazawa 1-day free pass** (金沢市内1日フリー乗車券) costs **¥800 for adults and ¥400 for children**. With four rides at ¥220, it already pays for itself.

- **Where to buy:** the Hokutetsu counter near platform 7 at Kanazawa Station East Exit, the Hokutetsu ticket center at the West Exit, some hotels, or as a digital ticket on your phone through the city's [Norimasshi Kanazawa](https://norimasshi.city.kanazawa.ishikawa.jp/) service. **You can't buy it on the bus.**
- **What it covers:** the Loop Bus, the weekend Shopping Bus, the Flat Bus, and local Hokutetsu and JR buses within central Kanazawa.
- **What it doesn't cover:** the Light-Up Bus, sightseeing tour buses and highway buses.
- It is valid only on the date printed on it. Show it to the driver when you get off.
- Showing the pass also gets you discounts at a number of museums and attractions.

## Other buses you might see

- **Kanazawa Shopping Bus:** runs on weekends, holidays and some holiday periods, also from platform 7. It goes through Korinbo, Katamachi and Hirosaka. ¥220 per ride.
- **Kanazawa Flat Bus** (金沢ふらっとバス): small city buses on short neighborhood routes, ¥100 per ride, paid when you get on.
- **Regular Hokutetsu city buses:** many of them also run between the station and Korinbo, and they keep running after the Loop Bus stops. On these, you get on at the **back door**, take a small numbered ticket (*seiri-ken*) if you are paying cash, and pay at the front when you get off. The fare depends on your numbered ticket, shown on the screen at the front.

## How to get off

Press the **stop button** when your stop is next. Then wait until the bus has fully stopped before standing up. Drivers are patient, so there is no need to rush.

## Say it in Japanese

> **Kenrokuen ni ikimasu ka?** (ken-roh-koo-en nee ee-kee-mass kah) — "Does this go to Kenrokuen?"

Swap in any place name: *Higashi Chaya ni ikimasu ka?*, *Katamachi ni ikimasu ka?*

> **Tsugi de orimasu.** (tsoo-gee deh oh-ree-mass) — "I'm getting off at the next stop."

> **Ichinichi joushaken o kudasai.** (ee-chee-nee-chee joh-shah-ken oh koo-dah-sigh) — "A 1-day pass, please."

> **Kore de ii desu ka?** (koh-reh deh ee dess kah) — "Is this OK?" (holding up your card or pass)

## Getting back at night

The Loop Bus finishes early. The last right-loop bus leaves Korinbo at about **18:24**, and the last left-loop bus leaves Katamachi at about **18:10**. After that you have two options:

- **Regular Hokutetsu city buses** run later between Korinbo and the station. Their last times vary by route and day, so check before you start your evening. The [Hokutetsu timetable search](https://www.hokutetsu.co.jp/route-bus/timetable/) or a map app with transit directions will show you.
- **Taxis** are the easiest choice late at night. From Katamachi to the station takes about ten minutes by car, depending on traffic.

My [guide to Kanazawa's nightlife districts](/articles/kanazawa-nightlife-areas/) has more on getting around after dark.

## A one-day route by bus

Here is an easy way to see the main sights with the 1-day pass:

1. **Kanazawa Station, platform 7** → right loop → **Musashigatsuji / Omicho Market** for breakfast or an early lunch.
2. Right loop → **Hashibacho** for the Higashi Chaya district.
3. Right loop → **Kenrokuen-shita** for the garden and Kanazawa Castle Park.
4. Walk to the **21st Century Museum**, or ride one stop to Hirosaka.
5. Right loop → **Korinbo**, then walk to the Nagamachi samurai district.
6. In the evening, walk to Kiguramachi or Katamachi for dinner, and take a taxi or a city bus back to the station.

That's at least four rides, so the ¥800 pass covers it. For the full plan with timing tips, see [Kanazawa for First-Timers](/articles/kanazawa-first-time-itinerary/).
